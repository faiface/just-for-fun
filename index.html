How to cook

  • Drag food and containers around. Pans, pots, plates and bowls live on the wall — drag them down to the counter or the stove.
  • Pour by holding a jar, bottle or container over another container. Pouring starts after a moment; let go when you have enough. The amount is shown as you pour.
  • Eggs: drop one into a bowl or pan, then click it to crack it. A boiled egg can be clicked to peel.
  • Utensils hang on the wall. Click one to pick it up, then: spoon / whisk — hold the mouse on a pot or bowl to stir or whisk · spatula — click food in a pan to flip it, hold to stir · tasting spoon — click a dish to taste it · knife (on the cutting board) — click or hold on food on the board; more chops = finer pieces. Right-click or Esc puts it down.
  • Stove: click the right half of a knob to turn the heat up, the left half to turn it down (or scroll).
  • Sink: put a pot in the sink and click the tap to fill it. Hold a pot over the empty sink to drain the liquid — the solids stay in.
  • Toaster: drop bread in, press the lever. The dial sets how dark.
  • Compost: drop food on it, or drop a container on it to empty it.
  • Serve: put the finished dish on the serving tray and ring the bell. Your guest only judges the food — not the steps you took.
  • Hover over anything to see what it is, how hot it is and how cooked it is. Use the time buttons to speed up waiting.
